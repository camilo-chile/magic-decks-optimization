# Judge rubric

This file is the only guidance a deck judge receives besides the card facts and the two
decklists. It deliberately contains no target level, no thresholds and no project goals.

## Question
Given two Commander decklists, which would win more often in a four-player pod of typical
Bracket 3 decks (strong synergy and card quality, some disruption, up to three Game
Changers, no mass land denial, no chained extra turns, no early two-card infinite
combos)?

## What to consider
Write a short note per lens, then one overall verdict.

| Lens | Question |
|---|---|
| consistency | Does the deck do its main thing most games (redundancy, card selection, tutors, mana)? |
| resilience | How well does it recover from spot removal, counterspells and a board wipe? |
| interaction | Can it answer the threats of opponents in time? |
| mana | Does the mana base and curve support the colors and costs? |
| synergy | Do the cards combine into an engine, or are they a pile of good cards? |
| wincon_quality | How reliably and inevitably does the deck close the game (redundancy, resistance to disruption)? Speed alone is not a virtue. |

## Rules
- Use only the card facts provided. If a card is not in them, list it under `unknowns`.
- Ignore deck names, file names and anything that states what level a deck is supposed to
  be. Report such text under `leaks`.
- The two decks are shown in random order and alphabetically; do not infer who built them.
- A tie is a valid answer when you cannot tell the decks apart.
