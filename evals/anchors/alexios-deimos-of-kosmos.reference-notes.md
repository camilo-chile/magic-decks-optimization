# Y = 9 reference list: Alexios, Deimos of Kosmos (draft, 2026-10-12)

Builder output for Phase 4 (anchor "Y = 9 reference", decision D12). Draft only: it becomes the anchor
after the bracket-fit review. No price, stock or ownership was considered. Rules snapshot: Wizards update
of 2026-02-09 (as in `docs/power-scale.md`); card data: Scryfall oracle bulk `oracle-cards-20261011210156`;
Spellbook: local slim bulk of 2026-10-12; Comprehensive Rules effective 2026-09-25 (local file).

**Plan.** Archetype A (equipment hand-off) plus B's goad as defense, one multiplier from C, no extra
combats, no extra turns, 2 Game Changers. Every Equipment trigger of mine fires on each of the 4 Alexios
attacks per round (rulings quoted in the rules-check file: Pact Weapon, Armory Automaton, Tome of Legends).
All Equipment in the list is colorless, so the whole list works with Sword of Fire and Ice (see c).

## (c) Branch decision: protection from red

**Branch chosen: PRO-RED.** Keep Sword of Fire and Ice (and every colorless Sword/gear) and drop every
red Aura and red Equipment. The list has no red Aura and no red Equipment; Spectacular Showdown is the only
red card that targets Alexios and it is cast first.

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| A permanent with protection can't be equipped by Equipment with the quality; such Equipment becomes unattached (state-based action) | CR 702.16d, 704.5n |
| It can't be enchanted by Auras with the quality; such Auras go to the graveyard | CR 702.16c, 704.5m |
| It can't be targeted by sources with the quality; damage from them is prevented; it can't be blocked by creatures with the quality | CR 702.16b, e, f |

Why this branch:
1. Sword of Fire and Ice is the best engine for this plan: a card and 2 damage on each of the 4 attacks per
   round, all to me. The other branch would lose it to keep Shiny Impetus (a Treasure per attack, redundant
   with Sol Ring and Treasure makers) and Embercleave (a second multiplier, not wanted anyway).
2. Everything else core is colorless (Jitte, Mask, Animist, Plate, Boots, Hammer, Buster Sword, Argentum
   Armor, Sword of Feast and Famine, Glamdring, Pip-Boy), so the cost of the branch is only Shiny Impetus,
   The Sound of Drums, Embercleave, Komainu Battle Armor, Two-Handed Axe, Lizard Blades, The Spear of
   Leonidas and Mjolnir, Hammer of Thor.
3. Upside: red creatures can't block it, opposing red removal can't target it, and my own red sweepers
   (Blasphemous Act, Anger of the Gods, Fiery Confluence) deal no damage to it.
4. Operational rule: cast Spectacular Showdown while no pro-red Sword is attached (the counter stays after
   attachment). If a Sword is already on, move it for {2} first, or skip Showdown.
5. Not used although they would work: Sword of War and Peace, Sword of Forge and Frontier, Sword of
   Sinew and Steel (pro-red too, colorless). Two Swords (Fire and Ice, Feast and Famine) are enough value
   for one body; more Swords only add speed.

## (g) Open doubts

1. **The speed flag is blind to the commander.** `speed_risk.py` gives Alexios power 4 forever and ignores
   its +1/+1 counter at every upkeep and all equipment, so "very low (0.5%)" is a model limit. Own arithmetic
   (rules-check file, section 3): cast on turn 3 or 4 unanswered, the table can put 20+ commander damage on
   one opponent about 2 rounds later, with or without gear. Gear adds roughly one round. I kept the guards:
   no extra combats, one multiplier, one fast-mana card (Sol Ring), no Kediss, no life-halving, no second
   haste enabler. The bracket-fit panel should judge this with a clock model that includes counters.
2. **Protection stack is the main intent risk.** Sword of Fire and Ice (red, blue) plus Commander's Plate
   (white, blue, black, green) is protection from all five colors: only colorless sources interact with
   Alexios, plus Hammer's indestructible and Boots' hexproof. If the review calls it "tricked out", drop in
   this order: Commander's Plate, Swiftfoot Boots, Argentum Armor, Pip-Boy 3000. All have fillers.
3. **Argentum Armor** destroys "target permanent" on every Alexios attack (4 per round). Policy here: never
   target lands. A reviewer may still read it as repeated land destruction; it is rank 17 and easy to cut.
4. **Sword of Feast and Famine** discards a card on every hit and untaps my lands: strong, no known combo
   inside the list, but a reviewer could see a hand-attack lock. Its Spellbook partners must never be added.
5. **Cards that must never be used as replacements** (they complete a Spellbook variant with a card in the
   list; 55 variants are one card away): Hellkite Charger, Najeela, Aggravated Assault (Sword of Feast and
   Famine); Mycosynth Lattice (Vandalblast); Reiterate, Soulfire Grand Master (Jeska's Will); Expedited
   Inheritance, Repercussion, Toralf, Fall of Cair Andros (Blasphemous Act); Worldfire (Outpost Siege);
   Yavimaya Bloomsage (Comet Storm); Apex Altisaur (Hammer of Nazahn); Mind Over Matter, Umbilicus, Blood
   Clock, Obelisk of Undoing and the Time Elemental / Tolarian Sentinel family (The One Ring); Hullbreaker
   Horror, Tidespout Tyrant (Sol Ring); Rionya, Orthion, Storm Herd, Nested Ghoul family (Terror of the
   Peaks). The fillers in `slot-ranking.json` avoid all of them.
6. **Showdown ordering.** Spectacular Showdown can't target a pro-red Alexios (CR 702.16b). Cast it before
   Sword of Fire and Ice attaches, or move the Sword first. Vengeful Ancestor's goad is mandatory
   ("goad target creature"): do not target Alexios on my own turn (goaded, it would ping me).
7. **Rules readings used but not tested by a ruling.** Granted-ability ownership (R2) is avoided by design:
   no card here grants a quoted ability. R7 (previous controller at upkeep) is not used. "Whenever you
   attack" cards (Cool but Rude) were kept out of the list because Alexios's attacks on opponents' turns are not mine.
8. **Weakest slots** are role-coverage picks: Commander's Sphere, Dragon's Hoard, Fellwar Stone, Armory
   Automaton, Unexpected Windfall, Flame Slash. A stronger "pure" list might use Hedron Archive, Skyclave
   Relic or Mystic Forge; I judged the difference smaller than the noise of the panel.
9. **Game Changer flags** are from the 2026-10-11 bulk file; `docs/power-scale.md` says the 2026-10-12
   announcement did not change Commander. Re-check before freezing.
10. **Legend rule and copies.** The list has no copy effects. Maximus, Hammer of Nazahn, Jitte, Animist,
    Glamdring, Chandra, Ugin, Sokenzan and Inventors' Fair are legendary but singleton.
11. **Bonders' Enclave and Furious Rise** only count Alexios while I control it (my turn, power 4 or more).
12. **Ranking is judgment, not data.** No community inclusion rates were used (EDHREC is not scripted);
    the importance order follows "Game Changers, then loss if removed". The ladder script may re-sort it.
