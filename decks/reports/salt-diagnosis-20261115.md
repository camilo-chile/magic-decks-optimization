## 6. Reading (main session, Opus 5.5 high, 2026-11-15)

- **Ezio.** The whole drop (6.5 to 4.6) is the Ezio + Magister Sphinx line: Win conditions 424 to 74, structural ceiling "no clear win path". Every other pillar held or improved (Interaction 139 to 173, tutors 12 to 14). Putting Sphinx back for Eivor returns 6.5 (bracket 3). Salt calls the line "wins the game outright"; in play it eliminates one opponent (Sphinx sets life to 10, then an Assassin connects and Ezio's {W}{U}{B}{R}{G} is paid), so Salt overrates it. Real power: a near wash. Eivor costs {B}{B} through Ezio's freerunning and brings haste and trample; Sphinx costs 7 (no freerunning, not an Assassin) but is a second life setter for route 1 and a flier (team evasion was a flagged gap). Bracket check on the Sphinx list: PASS (floor 3, 0 Game Changers).
- **Kilo.** Iter 2 to Iter 3 (4.2 to 3.7): Manabase Quality 94 to 82 (Path of Ancestry, Rustvale Bridge and The Mycosynth Gardens replaced Buried Ruin and two basics), Interaction 125 to 108, and a new "incidental combos" penalty; Win conditions rose. The land change was deliberate (Kilo on turn 3 from 80.9 % to 93.4 % by turn 5 in the builder's simulation). Base - Kilo (the precon with Kilo as commander) reads 3.8, almost equal to Iter 3, while the real-power estimates differ by about 1.5 points (precon 4.0-4.5, Iter 3 5.6-6.0): Salt does not track this deck. Its remaining complaints (no finisher, no tutors) are the Spanish collection's ceiling.
