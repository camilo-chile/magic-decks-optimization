# Assassin's Creed commander screening to replace Alexios, 2026-10-18

Why: Alexios (a Berserker, not an Assassin) measured 4.1 at 24,822 CLP, 5.9 at 97,602 and 6.8 at 197,392
(`decks/reports/round2-measured-20261018.md`). The owner wants an Assassin's Creed assassin commander instead.

## Owner rules (2026-10-18, new collection export `ManaBox_Collection (18-10-2026).csv` and binder `Assassin's Creed`)
- The binder `Assassin's Creed` (49 copies, taken from his Standard decks, 4 good-quality proxies: Ezio Auditore da
  Firenze, Altaïr Ibn-La'Ahad, Edward Kenway, Basim Ibn Ishaq) is NOT free for the Commander decks.
- Exception (the only one, after the owner changed his mind about the two Swords later the same night): the commander
  may be any creature of the collection, the proxies included (his friends' rules allow proxies as commanders only).
  Sword of Feast and Famine and Sword of Light and Shadow (empty color identity) are not free: to be used they are bought.
  He may prefer to buy the real commander card; if so, the purchase can stay outside the budget (his call, by price).
- Free cards stay the previous ones: binders English Cards, Spanish Cards, Lands (571 unique cards) minus what the
  other decks use.

## Reading
Ezio is the best fit: its core is cheap legendary Assassins, a quarter of the collection's free creatures are Assassins,
the commander is free (proxy) and the mana base is cheap despite five colors. Shao Jun (RU) is the cheap fallback. Kassandra
is too expensive, Basim and Edward collide with Azula's colors, and the monocolor assassins have no reference data.
These are screening estimates: the real test is a ladder measured on Commander Salt.
