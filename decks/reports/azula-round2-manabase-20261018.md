# Azula round 2 on Commander Salt: the mana base is the lever, 2026-10-18

Two lists hosted on the owner's Moxfield (unlisted) and scored by hand on Commander Salt, scoring version 2026.4195.
For each list the cards Salt scored (its "Export list") hash identically to our file
(`decks/proposals/measured.json`).

Reading:
- The round-2 ladder bought spells before lands (a judgment: "lands added little", read from a stretch of round 1 that
  also bought spells). Its step 24 scored 1.1 below the round-1 list of the same cost although three of its four pillars
  were equal or better. The one clear difference was the Manabase Quality (32 % against 68 %).
- The controlled test changed only lands (steps 30-38 of the round-2 ladder: Sulfur Falls, Shivan Reef, Sunken
  Hollow, Darkwater Catacombs, Shadowblood Ridge, Smoldering Marsh, Dragonskull Summit, Drowned Catacomb, Exotic
  Orchard, instead of basics and the tapped lands). It cost 7,155 CLP and added **+1.4 power**: the best power per CLP
  measured so far, and it ends above round 1's 6.6 at about the same cost.
- Salt also flagged, as small penalties, "combos without consistency" (four combo lines that share few pieces) and
  "wincon combos with no tutors" (-0.05 cap); a tutor or two is worth a slot.
- Caveat: one controlled pair on one deck. Salt's formula is not published, so the size of the effect on Toph and
  Alexios is to be confirmed with their own checkpoints.

Consequences:
- Every ladder puts the cheap untapped duals of the deck's colors (check, pain, filter, battle lands at about 500 to
  1,200 CLP) at the front of the purchase order and removes tapped lands early.
- The Toph and Alexios agents were told mid-run (round 2); Azula's ladder needs a round-3 reorder, to be folded into the
  round 3 planned after the wishlists are priced.
- Round 1's original order stays the control. The round-2 base improvements (owned cards instead of the former locked
  ones) are probably worth keeping, to be confirmed by measurement after the reorder.
