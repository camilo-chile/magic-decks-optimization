## Reading
- Every deck is at 7.0 or more by about 50,000 CLP, a full point above the friend's 6.1. The Manabase Quality lesson of
  round 2 held: 13 to 16 cheap untapped duals (about 7,000 to 10,000 CLP) put Azula and Ezio at 94 to 96 % or more.
- Power then plateaus (Azula 7.1 from 49,592 to 98,862; Ezio 7.0 to 7.1 from 45,872 to 141,322; Toph 7.4 to 7.5 from
  49,142 on). More lands or a few hundred thousand CLP of staples add 0.0 to 0.1. Salt's reason is structural: its ceiling
  line says "wincon combos with no tutors to assemble them" (Azula -0.12 to -0.35) or "sparse, slow combo package". Toph's
  ceiling vanished at step 30 (seven tutors that reach its win lines).
- Expensive late buys do not help and can hurt (Toph 148,907: 7.4 below 97,537: 7.5; Ezio 70,872: 6.9). Salt differences
  of 0.1 are within its noise (the same list scored twice moved salt by 0.2, power not at all).
- Under the parity rule the weakest deck set the level and the curve was flat. The owner dropped the rule (2026-10-19): with the
  highest total power the knee is about 145,000 CLP (Toph 7.4 for 49,142 CLP): see `decks/reports/budget-curve.md`.
