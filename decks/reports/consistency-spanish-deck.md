# Consistency gate: spanish-deck (PASS)

Plan: Kilo is tapped once or twice a round (a haste attack when a lane is open, otherwise at home: Relic of Legends at instant speed, station on Inspirit or Uthros, waterbend and improvise; North Pole Patrol is his only untapper) and Strionic Resonator and Lithoform Engine copy the trigger, so he proliferates the charge, +1/+1 and stun counters that about 20 rocks, engines and creatures place; Solar Array and Lux Artillery make artifacts enter with three or four more counters (any-color mana pays a fourth and fifth color). Surge Conductor, Tekuthal, Thrummingbird and the proliferate spells multiply the same counters into mana, cards and removal, and the deck closes against the whole table with the first noncombat finisher it reaches: Darksteel Reactor (20 charge counters, funnelled by Moxite Refinery, Resourceful Defense and Deepglow Skate), Lux Artillery (10 to each opponent every end step, 20 when the trigger is copied or The Mycosynth Gardens copies it) or the three-card loop Lux Artillery + Crystalline Crawler + Meticulous Excavation.
Backup: The same artifact board attacks: Steel Overseer and proliferate grow the artifact creatures, Cyberdrive Awakener turns every noncreature artifact into a 4/4 flyer for one alpha strike, Kappa Cannoneer is unblockable, and Alibou shoots X at any target each time an artifact creature attacks (X = my tapped artifacts).

## Win routes

- Darksteel Reactor funnel (alternate): darksteel reactor, deepglow skate, moxite refinery, resourceful defense. Against three opponents: At 20 charge counters the Reactor wins the game against all three opponents at once. It gains one each upkeep and one per proliferate (two with Tekuthal), enters with three or four through Solar Array, and takes two per combat from Inspirit and one per turn from Coretapper. The funnels finish it: Moxite Refinery turns X counters of any kind on one artifact or creature into X charge counters (Inspirit after Kilo has stationed it, Crystalline Crawler, Everflowing Chalice), Resourceful Defense moves the charge counters of one permanent at instant speed and catches the counters of any permanent that leaves, Deepglow Skate doubles it (10 becomes 20; 5 becomes 20 with Strionic Resonator copying the trigger). It is indestructible.
- Lux Artillery burn and the Crawler loop (noncombat): lux artillery, meticulous excavation, reckless fireweaver. Against three opponents: With 30 counters among my artifacts and creatures Lux Artillery deals 10 to EACH opponent at every one of my end steps; Strionic Resonator or Lithoform Engine copies the trigger, and Phyrexian Metamorph or The Mycosynth Gardens becomes a second Artillery, so a 40-life table dies in two end steps. Reckless Fireweaver deals 1 to each opponent for every artifact that enters (about 15 to 20 over a game). The loop: with Lux Artillery out, Crystalline Crawler cast with four colors of mana enters with eight counters (converge plus sunburst); remove them for eight mana of any color, pay {2}{W} to Meticulous Excavation to return Crawler, recast it with four colors: one mana gained per loop, unlimited artifact casts and entries on my turn. Fireweaver then kills the table, Surge Conductor proliferates the Reactor to 20, and Jhoira, Uthros, Thought Monitor or Etched Oracle draw the deck to find them.
- Artifact alpha strike and Alibou (combat): alibou, ancient witness, cyberdrive awakener, kappa cannoneer. Against three opponents: Cast from land mana, Cyberdrive Awakener makes every untapped noncreature artifact (8 to 14 late, artifact lands included) a 4/4 and gives every artifact creature flying for the turn: 30 to 50 flying damage split over the table after Lux Artillery, Fireweaver and Alibou have softened it. Kappa Cannoneer is unblockable every turn an artifact enters. Alibou deals X to any target and scries X whenever an artifact creature attacks (X is 4 to 8 with the rocks tapped): it kills the best blocker of each opponent in turn, or finishes a player.
