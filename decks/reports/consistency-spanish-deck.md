# Consistency gate: spanish-deck (PASS)

Plan: Kilo is tapped once or twice per round (a haste attack when the lane is open, otherwise at home and at instant speed through Relic of Legends and North Pole Patrol's waterbend, plus station and improvise; North Pole Patrol, his only untapper, gives the second tap) and Strionic Resonator and Lithoform Engine copy his trigger, so he proliferates three to four times a round the charge, +1/+1, poison and stun counters that about 20 rocks, engines and creatures place; Surge Conductor, Thrummingbird, Tekuthal and the proliferate spells multiply the same counters into mana, cards and threats. The deck closes with whichever noncombat finisher it reaches first: Darksteel Reactor (an alternate win against the whole table, funnelled by Resourceful Defense, Moxite Refinery and Deepglow Skate), Kappa Cannoneer if bought (one poison counter on each opponent that every later proliferate grows to 10) or Lux Artillery (10 to each opponent every end step, 20 with its trigger copied).
Backup: The same countered artifact board attacks: Cyberdrive Awakener turns every noncreature artifact into a 4/4 flyer for the turn and Titan Forge adds 9/9 Golems; Lux Artillery's end-step damage and Fireweaver pings soften the table first.

## Win routes

- Darksteel Reactor funnel (alternate): darksteel reactor, deepglow skate, moxite refinery, resourceful defense. Against three opponents: The Reactor wins the game against all three opponents at 20 charge counters. It gains one each upkeep and one per proliferate (Kilo, Surge Conductor, Thrummingbird, the proliferate spells; doubled by Tekuthal; copied by Strionic Resonator and Lithoform Engine); Coretapper and Inspirit add charge counters; the funnels finish it: Resourceful Defense moves every counter of one permanent onto it at instant speed (and the counters of any permanent of mine that leaves), Moxite Refinery converts X counters of any kind into X charge counters, Deepglow Skate doubles it (10 becomes 20). It is indestructible, so destroy-wipes do not stop it.
- Lux Artillery burn (noncombat): lux artillery, meticulous excavation, reckless fireweaver. Against three opponents: Lux Artillery deals 10 to EACH opponent at every end step once 30 counters sit on my artifacts and creatures; Fireweaver pings each opponent per artifact entry; Strionic Resonator or Lithoform Engine copying the end-step trigger, or Phyrexian Metamorph copying Lux, doubles it (two end steps kill a 40-life table). With Lux, Crystalline Crawler (six counters in three colors) and Surge Conductor on the battlefield (its proliferate on the entry makes seven counters, exactly the {2}{W} plus {4} the loop costs; Tekuthal makes it net +1 mana), Meticulous Excavation loops Crawler for infinite artifact entries and proliferates on my turn: Fireweaver kills the table, or the Reactor and the poison counters reach their totals.
- Artifact alpha strike (combat): cyberdrive awakener, titan forge. Against three opponents: Cast with land mana (rocks tapped for mana cannot attack), Cyberdrive makes every untapped noncreature artifact (8 to 14 late) a 4/4 and every artifact creature a flyer for the turn: 30 to 50 flying damage split over the table after Lux and Fireweaver have softened it; Titan Forge adds 9/9s that also hold off big creatures.
