# Phase 8, step 1: the model and what it showed (2026-10-18)

Code: `scripts/allocation_model.py` (library, 6 tests in `tests/test_allocation_model.py`) and
`scripts/price_candidates.py` (the owner runs it; writes `data/model/`). Inputs: merged prices
`data/prices/merged-20261017T2337Z.json`, inventory `data/cache/collection_inventory.json` (690 unique cards,
1,364 copies), candidates `decks/candidates/` with their scores in `decks/candidates/scores.json`.

## Rules the model applies
- Cards are matched by Scryfall oracle ID (a list, a store and the collection may spell a name differently).
- Owned copies are free; one physical copy sits in one deck (D5), so pricing the 3 decks in turn shares each
  copy once (`claimed`). Japanese copies are not usable (English or Spanish only).
- Otherwise the cheapest available English or Spanish offer, any printing and finish; Spanish wins a tie.
- Basic lands are free (the owner holds 233). Cards with no stock anywhere are reported, never priced.
- Shipping: 5,000 CLP per store used, reported, outside the budget.
- Power is never computed: it is the Commander Salt score of that exact list. A changed list has no power until
  it is scored on the site.

## What the first run showed (each list priced alone, owned copies free)
| Deck | Cheapest list that passes Layer A at Bracket 3 | Cost CLP | Best-power passing list | Cost CLP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alexios (owns 12 of the cards) | ec03cc3a, power 7.5 | 790,862 | same | 790,862 |
| Toph (owns 30-43) | 9354c58f, power 7.4 | 266,470 | same | 266,470 |
| Azula (owns 17-23) | 0653e6b7, power 7.2 | 497,800 | 963a6e62, 7.4 | 546,097 |

- Even the cheapest passing real lists cost about 1.55 million CLP together, against a ceiling of 300,000. So the
  budget curve cannot be made of whole candidates: each budget needs lists adapted from the candidates (strategy
  kept, expensive cards swapped for cheaper or owned ones). Those lists have no site score until scored by hand.
- Cost is concentrated: the 5 most expensive cards are 30-57 % of a list (for example Enduring Curiosity 116,560,
  The One Ring 124,000, Mox Opal 252,000, Plateau 329,000 and Taiga 241,060 in a collector printing).
- Candidates are not fully buyable: 0 to 8 of their cards have no stock in the 8 stores (Jeska's Will, Terra Eternal,
  Brass Squire, Valley Floodcaller, The Sound of Drums ...), a forced swap each.
- The owner's own cards are a large free pool: 391 non-basic cards fit Toph's identity, 385 fit Azula's and 157 fit
  Alexios (all owned and usable, one copy per deck).
- Locked cards held by the candidates: 2-8 of the 32 each (the owner's lists hold 17-18), so most locked cards must be
  inserted into the chosen lists.

## Next (step 2): the expert builds the lists
Opus 5.5 at extra-high (owner decision 2026-10-18), one agent per deck, from `data/model/pool-<slug>.json`, the
candidate lists and the Upgrades picks: a free base list from owned cards, then an ordered ladder of purchases (what
each added card does for the deck and its price), with the locked cards placed. The optimizer (step 3) cuts the ladders
at each budget (100,000 to 300,000 CLP, parity within 0.2) and checks every proposed list with Layer A, color identity,
singleton, stock, shared copies and lock coverage. The owner scores the resulting lists on Commander Salt.
