## 4. Required report changes, ordered by severity

1. (Medium) Section 6, Stasis Coffin row: replace "opponents cannot target or damage you" with the ruling-based scope (claim 46) and state that protection ends on your own turn; say the Spellbook "Lock" label is not a rules lock. This is the argument the bracket-fit review would use to keep the Coffin.
2. (Medium) Section 6, Mindslaver row and section 2 D: "one opponent's next turn per round, chosen each time", not "one opponent on each of their turns"; the floor-4 call stays a policy call.
3. (Medium) Section 1 weakness "Land hate hits artifacts": say artifact-lands become Mountains that tap for {R}, lose their ETB and own abilities, basic lands are unaffected (claim 12). Add Zhao's no-counter "Nonbasic lands enter tapped" as a slow-down.
4. (Medium) Section 1, earthbend row: replace "(or +2 counters on an existing one)" with "an already animated land gets +2 counters, haste and base 0/0, which can be a downgrade" (claim 7); add the "not returned from other zones" limit.
5. (Low-medium) Section 1 weakness on summoning sickness: add that earthbend grants haste, so earthbended artifacts tap at once; only creature artifacts not earthbended are sick (claim 11).
6. (Low-medium) Section 7 and section 5 Extra turns: note Greenwarden and Panharmonicon multiply Gonti's energy and artifact-enters damage; add Mossborn Hydra as a speed driver.
7. (Low) Section 5/6, Caged Sun: the loop needs a land that makes the chosen color; "mandatory" holds only then (claim 14).
8. (Low) Section 5 "Repeatable engines": Urza's Saga returns at chapter I and fetches again after two more turns, not each turn (claim 31).
9. (Low) Section 1 and 3.1: "on each cast" should read "when it enters"; add that artifacts already in play do not trigger when Toph arrives (claim 1).
10. (Low) Section 8 item 1: all rules-background items except claims 4, 14 (draw result), 36, 37 and 43 can be marked confirmed with the rulings above; delete "Scryfall rulings returned HTTP 403". Item 8: Hour of Revelation discount note.
11. (Minor) Section 3.3 Tireless Tracker: "every artifact you cast is a Clue" should read "each artifact entering makes a Clue token" (Tracker 2024-02-02: Clue tokens are not lands, so no loop).
