# Commander Brackets: the 2026-02-09 rules, pinned down for code

*Independently fact-checked twice (2026-10-10 and 2026-10-11); the corrections from both checks are applied below. Not re-verifiable by WebFetch in the second pass: the card names and expectation text on the Wizards format page (JS-rendered), the 2026-03-23, 2026-05-18 and 2026-06-29 Banned and Restricted pages (only 2026-08-10 was read as primary), and the Scryfall docs pages (HTTP 403).*

The current official Commander Bracket rules are the layered result of four Wizards of the Coast articles (2025-02-11, 2025-04-22, 2025-10-21, 2026-02-09), and the system is still labelled **beta** on the official format page as of 2026-10-10. There are five brackets (1 Exhibition, 2 Core, 3 Upgraded, 4 Optimized, 5 cEDH). Only three things are exact and checkable by code: the **Game Changers count per bracket (0 / 0 / up to 3 / unlimited / unlimited)**, the **53-card Game Changers list**, and the fact that **tutors carry no bracket restriction since 2025-10-21**. Mass land denial, extra turns and two-card infinite combos are official categories with prose guidance but no official card lists and no numeric thresholds, so a deterministic checker can only compute a *minimum bracket implied by contents*, never an exact bracket. Commander Spellbook's `POST /estimate-bracket` is the best available automated signal, but it emits one of seven letter tags (never Bracket 5), uses its own thresholds, and disagrees with Wizards in a few specific places listed below. Neither Scryfall nor Commander Spellbook publishes an official MCP server; both are plain, key-less REST APIs, and the community MCP servers are single-maintainer hobby projects. Wizards' next Banned & Restricted announcement is dated 2026-10-12, two days after this snapshot (that is the general announcement page; Commander changes are published separately), so a `version`/`as_of` field in the rules snapshot is essential.

## Four dated Wizards articles define the current bracket rules, and the beta tag is still on

Wizards has published no single consolidated rules table; the current rules are the 2025-02-11 launch deck-building lines amended by later articles. The primary sources, all on magic.wizards.com, are the launch article ([2025-02-11](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/introducing-commander-brackets-beta)), the first update ([2025-04-22](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-april-22-2025)), the second update that added per-bracket expectations and turn benchmarks ([2025-10-21](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-october-21-2025)), and the third update plus a Banned and Restricted announcement ([2026-02-09](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-february-9-2026), [B&R](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-banned-and-restricted-february-9-2026)). The living [Commander format page](https://magic.wizards.com/en/formats/commander) says "Commander Brackets are currently in beta" and mirrors the 2025-10-21 names and expectations. The 2026-02-09 article promised no bracket-level changes until at least one 2026 MagicCon had been observed, said the beta tag might drop "later this year," and anticipated an update "likely in May or June." No such article was found as of 2026-10-10; the 2026-03-23, 2026-05-18, 2026-06-29 and 2026-08-10 Banned and Restricted pages had no Commander section ([2026-08-10](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/banned-and-restricted-august-10-2026)). Secondary sources dated 2026-09-03 and 2026-10-09 still describe the system as beta ([EDHREC](https://edhrec.com/articles/should-the-bracket-system-move-out-of-beta/), [Scrollvault](https://scrollvault.net/guides/commander-brackets.html)). The state to snapshot is therefore "2026-02-09 beta."

The numbering and expectation text below comes from the 2025-10-21 article and the format page ([Wizards 2025-10-21](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-october-21-2025), [format page](https://magic.wizards.com/en/formats/commander)). The turn benchmark reads "you should expect to be able to play at least N turns before you win or lose," and Wizards clarifies that for Bracket 3 this means the seventh turn is when a game ending would be acceptable.

| # | Name | Expectation (abbreviated, from Wizards) | Min. turns before win/lose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Exhibition | Prioritizes a goal, theme or idea over power; win conditions "highly thematic or substandard"; flexible card legality by pod agreement | 9 |
| 2 | Core | "unoptimized and straightforward"; win conditions "incremental, telegraphed on the board, and disruptable"; low pressure | 8 |
| 3 | Upgraded | Strong synergy and card quality, can disrupt opponents; Game Changers are value engines and game-ending spells; win "deployed in one big turn from hand" | 6 |
| 4 | Optimized | Lethal, consistent, fast; Game Changers are fast mana, snowballing engines, free disruption, tutors; "explosive and powerful" | 4 |
| 5 | cEDH | Built for the cEDH metagame; efficiency and consistency; "players prioritize victory over all else" | none ("could end on any turn") |

Three framing rules matter for any implementation. First, intent outranks the checklist: Wizards states "Intent is the most important part of the bracket system," that you can always "bracket decks up," and that violating an expectation once "isn't something where... it's immediately out of the bracket" ([2025-04-22](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-april-22-2025), [2025-10-21](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-october-21-2025)). Second, Brackets 4 and 5 have identical hard rules ("no restrictions (other than the banned list)") and differ only by mindset and metagame ([2025-02-11](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/introducing-commander-brackets-beta)), so a checker should treat 5 as user-declared. Third, Bracket 2 is no longer defined as "the average precon": the launch wording was explicitly removed on 2025-10-21 ([Wizards 2025-10-21](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-october-21-2025)). As of 2026-02-09 an extra bracket (a split of Bracket 3) was still under discussion and had not been adopted ([2026-02-09](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-february-9-2026)).

## Game Changers are the only hard number; mass land denial, extra turns and combos stay judgment calls

The deck-building matrix below uses the launch wording ([2025-02-11](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/introducing-commander-brackets-beta)) with the tutor row replaced by the 2025-10-21 change. The format page confirms the Game Changer counts: "Bracket 1 and 2 decks exclude Game Changers. Bracket 3 allows for up to three Game Changers. Brackets 4 and 5 allow for unlimited Game Changers" ([format page](https://magic.wizards.com/en/formats/commander)). The turn benchmarks quoted in the table above are official; only the Bracket 1 and 2 extensions in the combo paragraph below are author inference.

| Bracket | Game Changers | Mass land denial | Extra turns | Two-card infinite combos | Tutors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | no intentional MLD | no intentional extra-turn cards | no intentional two-card infinite combos | no limit |
| 2 | 0 | no intentional MLD | low quantities, not chained or looped | no intentional two-card infinite combos | no limit |
| 3 | max 3 | none | low quantities, not chained or looped | no intentional *early-game* two-card infinite combos | no limit |
| 4 | unlimited | allowed | allowed | allowed | no limit |
| 5 | unlimited | allowed | allowed | allowed | no limit |

**Mass land denial** is defined as cards that "regularly destroy, exile, and bounce other lands, keep lands tapped, or change what mana is produced by **four or more lands per player** without replacing them," with Armageddon, Ruination, Sunder, Winter Orb and Blood Moon as examples, and "you should not expect to see these cards anywhere in Brackets 1-3" ([2025-02-11](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/introducing-commander-brackets-beta)). Wizards declined to publish an exact list when asked, with an answer that included "changing the tiny details of the system this early" ([2025-04-22](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-april-22-2025)). The "four or more lands" threshold is the only numeric anchor, so an MLD card list must be curated by the project. Accidental board states such as animated lands plus a sweeper are not violations; building to do it intentionally is ([2025-02-11](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/introducing-commander-brackets-beta)). Sundering Titan remains banned, and Wizards wrote, about looping it, that "that kind of land-denial strategy is not something welcome in Bracket 3" ([B&R 2026-02-09](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-banned-and-restricted-february-9-2026)).

**Extra turns** are allowed in Brackets 2 and 3 only in "low quantities" and "not intended to be chained in succession or looped"; Wizards' reasoning is that "in multiples, they begin to function like a combo deck," so the Edric chain-everything deck belongs in higher brackets ([2025-02-11](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/introducing-commander-brackets-beta)). **No numeric definition of "low quantities" exists anywhere in the official sources.** Extra-turn cards are not automatically Game Changers: Expropriate was removed from the list on 2025-10-21 ([Wizards 2025-10-21](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-october-21-2025)).

**Two-card infinite combos** are barred outright in Brackets 1 and 2. In Bracket 3 the launch text said "no intentional early-game two-card infinite combos" and described the target as "cheaply and in about the first six or so turns" ([2025-02-11](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/introducing-commander-brackets-beta)). The 2025-10-21 update replaced the vague "early-game" with the turn benchmark: combos should not "tend to happen in the first six turns," while holding a combo back deliberately does not make it acceptable, since "if a combo could frequently come up, it's not the best fit for that bracket" ([Wizards 2025-10-21](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-october-21-2025)). Wizards' test is whether a combo "tends to happen" early, and its anchor is that "someone's seventh turn" is when a game ending would be acceptable. The project's working convention, which is not Wizards' wording, is that a Bracket 3 two-card infinite combo should not be reachable on turns 1 through 6. Applying the same rule, a combo-free Bracket 2 ends no earlier than the ninth turn and Bracket 1 no earlier than the tenth (author inference). There is no official list of combos; Wizards gave "the brackets already do a good job of restricting combos" as its reason for removing Food Chain from the Game Changers list, not as a general statement of policy ([2025-10-21](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-october-21-2025)).

**Tutors** lost their bracket restriction on 2025-10-21: "we'd like to... remove the tutor restrictions from Commander Brackets entirely and rely on Game Changers to catch the most efficient tutors" ([Wizards 2025-10-21](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-october-21-2025)). The launch line "Tutors should be sparse" and the April question "What does 'a few tutors' mean?" are superseded ([2025-04-22](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-april-22-2025)). In practice many of the tutors that matter are Game Changers (not all: Demonic Consultation is not): Demonic Tutor, Vampiric Tutor, Imperial Seal, Enlightened Tutor, Mystical Tutor, Worldly Tutor, Gamble, Intuition, Gifts Ungiven, Survival of the Fittest, Crop Rotation and Natural Order are all on the current list ([format page](https://magic.wizards.com/en/formats/commander)).

Two further rules affect a checker. A Game Changer that is your commander counts toward the Bracket 3 limit of three, and the rule applies to the 99 equally ([2025-02-11](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/introducing-commander-brackets-beta)). Stax-style lock play and "tricked-out" decks without Game Changers should "bracket up" despite meeting the deck-building rules ([2025-04-22](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-april-22-2025)). No primary source says whether companions or maybeboard cards count toward the Game Changer cap.

## The Game Changers list holds 53 cards and Scryfall exposes it as a boolean

The list was 40 cards at launch (2025-02-11), 61 after 2025-04-22 (+18 added, +5 unbanned onto it, -2 removed), 51 after 2025-10-21 (-10), and **53 after 2026-02-09** (+Farewell, +Biorhythm, nothing removed) ([2025-02-11](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/introducing-commander-brackets-beta), [2025-04-22](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-april-22-2025), [2025-10-21](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-october-21-2025), [2026-02-09](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-february-9-2026)). The count was verified on 2026-10-10 through a live Scryfall search (`total_cards: 53`), the Scryfall Oracle Cards bulk file generated 2026-10-09T21:01:54Z, and playgroup.gg ([Scryfall API](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/search?q=is%3Agamechanger&unique=cards&order=name), [bulk-data](https://api.scryfall.com/bulk-data), [Playgroup.gg](https://playgroup.gg/commander/game-changers)). Wizards also stated that cards unbanned from Commander go onto the list by standard practice, with Lutri, the Spellchaser as the only exception ([2026-02-09](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-february-9-2026)).

| Blue | 10 | Consecrated Sphinx; Cyclonic Rift; Fierce Guardianship; Force of Will; Gifts Ungiven; Intuition; Mystical Tutor; Narset, Parter of Veils; Rhystic Study; Thassa's Oracle |

Groups follow color identity (for example Serra's Sanctum is listed under White and Gaea's Cradle under Green), and Wizards lists Tergrid by its front face only. The 53 names are those returned by [Scryfall](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/search?q=is%3Agamechanger&unique=cards&order=name) and agree with the grouping on the [official format page](https://magic.wizards.com/en/formats/commander). Cards removed and therefore no longer Game Changers are Trouble in Pairs and Trinisphere (2025-04-22), and Expropriate, Jin-Gitaxias Core Augur, Sway of the Stars, Vorinclex Voice of Hunger, Kinnan Bonder Prodigy, Urza Lord High Artificer, Winota Joiner of Forces, Yuriko the Tiger's Shadow, Deflecting Swat and Food Chain (2025-10-21). Sol Ring is deliberately absent ("Every deck gets exactly one Sol Ring") ([2025-02-11](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/introducing-commander-brackets-beta)).

For machine access, Wizards offers only HTML tabs with no JSON or CSV feed. The practical authority is Scryfall: the search `is:gamechanger` (documented in the [syntax guide](https://scryfall.com/docs/syntax)) and the nullable boolean card field `game_changer` ([Cards API](https://scryfall.com/docs/api/cards)). The form `game_changer:true` is **invalid** and returned HTTP 400 on 2026-10-10, while Draftsim's spelling `is:game-changer` also works and returns the same 53 cards (verified 2026-10-10) ([Scryfall API test](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/search?q=game_changer%3Atrue), [Draftsim](https://draftsim.com/mtg-edh-game-changers/)). For a pipeline, resolve the `oracle_cards` entry from `https://api.scryfall.com/bulk-data` each time (the file name embeds a timestamp, and entries now expose `jsonl_download_uri` rather than `download_uri`), download the roughly 24.6 MB `.jsonl.gz`, and filter `game_changer == true`, treating null as false ([bulk data docs](https://scryfall.com/docs/api/bulk-data)). Live API calls need explicit `User-Agent` and `Accept` headers and are limited to **2 requests per second** on `/cards/search`, `/cards/named`, `/cards/random` and `/cards/collection` and 10 per second elsewhere; a 429 triggers a 30-second block ([rate limits](https://scryfall.com/docs/api/rate-limits); the second check could only confirm these figures through secondary sources because the primary page returns 403 to WebFetch, so re-read it in a browser). Name matching must handle the double-faced Tergrid entry containing `//`. Scryfall may lag a Wizards change by hours to days (author inference), which matters because the 2026-10-12 announcement falls two days after this snapshot.

## Commander Spellbook's estimator is a seven-tag waterfall over flagged cards and assembled combos

Commander Spellbook is a community project (MIT-licensed code, "Not approved/endorsed by Wizards") and not a Wizards source ([About](https://commanderspellbook.com/about/)). Its API base is `https://backend.commanderspellbook.com`, unauthenticated, camelCase JSON, OpenAPI 3.0.3 version 7.1.9 at [`/schema/`](https://backend.commanderspellbook.com/schema/). The guidance is a descriptive `User-Agent`, "80 requests per minute should be a safe rate," and handling of 429 ([docs/api.md](https://github.com/SpaceCowMedia/commander-spellbook-backend/blob/master/docs/api.md)). The dataset is also published as a bulk file at [`https://json.commanderspellbook.com/variants.json.gz`](https://json.commanderspellbook.com/variants.json.gz) (about 29 MB, keys `timestamp`, `version`, `variants`, `aliases`; 114,450 variants on 2026-10-10), and maintainers ask clients not to page through the API to export data.

**Input.** `estimate-bracket` and `find-my-combos` take one decklist body, either `application/json` or `text/plain`; `card-list-from-text` is documented as `text/plain` only. The JSON shape is `{"main":[{"card":"Sol Ring","quantity":1}],"commanders":[{"card":"Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy","quantity":1}]}` with at most 600 main entries, 12 commanders and 256 characters per name ([serializers](https://github.com/SpaceCowMedia/commander-spellbook-backend/blob/master/backend/common/serializers.py)). Plain text takes one card per line with an optional leading `4` or `4x` and section markers such as `// Commander`, `// Sideboard` and `// Maybeboard`. A name may be an exact name, lower-cased or accent-stripped name, a Spellbook card number, or a Scryfall Oracle ID, and **unknown names are silently dropped** ([views/utils.py](https://github.com/SpaceCowMedia/commander-spellbook-backend/blob/master/backend/spellbook/views/utils.py)). Commanders are part of the card multiset; the optional query parameter `unknownCommanders=true` treats an empty commander list as unknown instead of none.

**Endpoints.** `POST /estimate-bracket` returns `{bracketTag, cards, combos}`. `cards` holds `ClassifiedCard` entries (`card`, `quantity`, `banned`, `gameChanger`, `massLandDenial`, `extraTurn`), and `combos` holds `ClassifiedVariant` entries (`combo` as a full `Variant`, `relevant`, `borderlineRelevant`, `arguablyTwoCard`, `definitelyTwoCard`, `speed`, `massLandDenial`, `extraTurn`, `lock`, `skipTurns`, `controlAllOpponents`, `controlSomeOpponents`) for fully assembled combos only ([estimate_bracket.py](https://github.com/SpaceCowMedia/commander-spellbook-backend/blob/master/backend/spellbook/views/estimate_bracket.py)). `POST /find-my-combos` takes the same body plus `count`, `groupByCombo`, `limit` (default and maximum 1000), `offset`, `ordering` and `q` (the website's Scryfall-style query language, with `bracket:` accepting 1-5 with comparators or a tag name). Its `results` is a single object (the live schema documents it as an object; only the wrapper class name ends in "List") with `identity` and six arrays: `included`, `includedByChangingCommanders`, `almostIncluded`, `almostIncludedByAddingColors`, `almostIncludedByChangingCommanders`, `almostIncludedByAddingColorsAndChangingCommanders`; "almost included" means missing at most one ingredient (a card copy or a template) ([find_my_combos.py](https://github.com/SpaceCowMedia/commander-spellbook-backend/blob/master/backend/spellbook/views/find_my_combos.py)). `GET /variants/` (page size 100 maximum) and `GET /variants/{id}/`, `GET /cards/` and `GET /cards/{id}/` (the latter exposing `gameChanger`, `tutor`, `massLandDenial`, `extraTurn`), `GET /card-list-from-url?url=` (Moxfield, Archidekt, Deckstats, TappedOut) and `GET /explain-query?q=` complete the useful set ([schema](https://backend.commanderspellbook.com/schema/)). A live call on 2026-10-10 with a Kinnan deck containing Thassa's Oracle, Demonic Consultation, Armageddon and Time Warp returned `bracketTag: "R"`, the Game Changer, mass-land-denial and extra-turn flags on the right cards, and one combo (variant 742-1295) with `relevant:true, definitelyTwoCard:true, speed:5`.

**Tag logic.** The tag is not a 1-5 number but one of `B` Banned, `R` Ruthless, `S` Spicy, `P` Powerful, `O` Oddball, `C` Core or `E` Exhibition. The numeric mapping in the model is R=4, S=3, P=3, O=2, C=2, E=1, B=null, and **Bracket 5 is never produced** ([models/variant.py](https://github.com/SpaceCowMedia/commander-spellbook-backend/blob/master/backend/spellbook/models/variant.py)). The function evaluates top to bottom and the first match wins:

```
B  any card banned (not commander-legal)
R  sum(qty of gameChanger cards) > 3  OR  sum(qty of extraTurn cards) >= 2
   OR any combo.extraTurn  OR any card/combo massLandDenial
   OR any combo.controlAllOpponents
   OR any combo with speed>=4 AND relevant AND definitelyTwoCard
S  any combo with speed>=4 AND ((relevant AND arguablyTwoCard)
                              OR (borderlineRelevant AND definitelyTwoCard))
   OR any combo.lock / skipTurns / controlSomeOpponents
P  any combo with speed>=3 AND relevant AND definitelyTwoCard
   OR any card.gameChanger
O  any combo with speed>=3 AND borderlineRelevant AND arguablyTwoCard
C  any card.extraTurn  OR any combo with speed>=2 AND relevant AND definitelyTwoCard
E  otherwise
```

`speed` is derived from `manaValueNeeded`: 0 gives 5, up to 4 gives 4, up to 6 gives 3, up to 8 gives 2, otherwise 1, then +1 when the editor-curated "is mana needed an accurate minimum" flag is false, giving a range of 1 to 6. That hidden flag is not in the public JSON, so `speed` is only readable from `/estimate-bracket` and cannot be recomputed from the bulk file. **There is no turn-number or "early game" field anywhere in the data.** Two-card status excludes library-zone cards and known commanders from the count, adds penalties for notable prerequisites, and defines `definitelyTwoCard` as sure plus arguable cards at most 2 and `arguablyTwoCard` as sure cards at most 2 with sure plus arguable at most 3 ([models/variant.py](https://github.com/SpaceCowMedia/commander-spellbook-backend/blob/master/backend/spellbook/models/variant.py)). The maintainer's own design note (edited 2026-01-12) calls Spicy and Oddball "corner case" buckets to be judged, roughly Bracket 3 or 4 and Bracket 2 or 3 ([issue #1002](https://github.com/SpaceCowMedia/commander-spellbook-backend/issues/1002)). Before migration 0059 (2026-01-27) the tags were R/S/P/O/PA/C (PA = Precon Appropriate, C = Casual); 0059 rewrote stored `C` as `E` (Exhibition) and `PA` as `C` (Core), so a pre-2026 `C` equals today's `E`. `B` was added in March 2026 ([migration 0059](https://github.com/SpaceCowMedia/commander-spellbook-backend/blob/master/backend/spellbook/migrations/0059_bracket_system_v3.py), [issue #1043](https://github.com/SpaceCowMedia/commander-spellbook-backend/issues/1043)).

## Where Spellbook and Wizards disagree, and which source a checker should trust

Spellbook's thresholds are the maintainers' own convention; no statement from Wizards or Spellbook validates them as a mapping to official brackets. The table lists the divergences that affect a rules checker, with the recommended handling.

| Topic | Wizards (primary) | Spellbook estimator or secondary source | Checker handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Game Changer count | 1 to 3 is Bracket 3; 4 or more is Bracket 4 (implied by "up to three") | `P` for any one GC; `R` only above 3 | Compute from the 53-card list: 1-3 gives floor 3, 4+ gives floor 4 |
| Game Changer list source | Official page, HTML tabs only | Spellbook has its own `gameChanger` flag per card; Scryfall `game_changer` mirrors Wizards | Use Scryfall/Wizards list; use Spellbook flag only as cross-check |
| Extra turns | Low quantities, not chained, in B2/B3; none in B1; "low quantities" undefined | One extra-turn card gives `C` (2); two or more gives `R` (4); any infinite-turns combo gives `R` | Single card floor 2; two or more is a review flag, with a project-set threshold (many tools use about 3). Project policy (power-scale v0.4): 2 or more is a reject |
| Mass land denial | Barred in B1-B3, defined by "four or more lands" | Any flagged MLD card or combo gives `R` (4) | Floor 4 using a curated list (Wizards supplies five examples only) |
| Two-card infinite combos | Barred in B1-B2; in B3 only if not reachable in the first six turns | Speed from mana value only; accurate combos needing 7-8 mana get `C` (2), 9+ get `E` (1), though B1/B2 bar these combos | Any intentional two-card infinite combo floors at 3 or higher; at 4 when it can assemble by about turn 6 |
| Turn semantics | Benchmark of at least six turns; first finish on the seventh turn | Never states a turn number; `speed >= 4` is a mana proxy | Treat Spellbook speed as advisory, not as a turn count |
| Tutors | No restriction since 2025-10-21 | Card flag `tutor` exists but the estimator ignores it | No bracket effect except via Game Changers |
| Bracket 5 | Defined only by mindset | Never emitted; `R` means "4 or above" | Never auto-assign 5 |
| Beta status | Still "currently in beta" | n/a | Carry `beta: true` and `as_of` in the snapshot |

Secondary-source drift also exists. Scrollvault phrases the Bracket 3 combo rule as allowed "only if it cannot come together before turn 6," a rounder cutoff than Wizards' "intentional early-game" plus seventh-turn wording ([Scrollvault](https://scrollvault.net/guides/commander-brackets.html)). The launch "quick recap" drops the words "intentional" and "infinite" while the same article's deck-building lines and Q&A keep it ([2025-02-11](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/introducing-commander-brackets-beta)). Stale launch-era claims persist, notably "Bracket 2 equals precon," which EDHREC reports many players still follow ([EDHREC](https://edhrec.com/articles/should-the-bracket-system-move-out-of-beta/)), the "sparse tutors" rule, and the 40- and 51-card lists. StarCityGames' recap of "average turns" misstates a minimum-turns expectation ([SCG](https://articles.starcitygames.com/magic-the-gathering/weeklymtg-announces-2027-limited-championship-updates-to-commander-brackets/)). The official format page's card names are present in its HTML (confirmed by an independent fact-check on 2026-10-10) and match Scryfall's 53 names exactly.

## No official MCP server exists; plain REST plus bulk data is the sound access path

Neither Scryfall nor Commander Spellbook publishes an official MCP server. The official MCP Registry has no entry under a Scryfall or Wizards namespace (only third-party `io.github.*` entries), no registry result for Commander Spellbook exists, the SpaceCowMedia GitHub organization contains no MCP repository, and the Scryfall API docs and the Spellbook backend root list no MCP ([Registry search](https://registry.modelcontextprotocol.io/v0/servers?search=scryfall), [SpaceCowMedia](https://github.com/SpaceCowMedia), [Spellbook backend](https://backend.commanderspellbook.com/)). Roughly 30 community MCP repos exist, but only a handful are substantial. The only one exposing a Spellbook-backed bracket estimate is [j4th/mtg-mcp-server](https://github.com/j4th/mtg-mcp-server) (MIT, 20 stars, 69 tools including `estimate_bracket`, `find_combos`, `combo_details`; tagged v3.0.0 on 2026-04-04, installed with `uvx mtg-mcp-server`), which looks stalled with two open August 2026 bugs (#68, #69), including 429 storms from uncapped Scryfall lookups ([issues](https://github.com/j4th/mtg-mcp-server/issues)). [gregario/mtg-oracle](https://github.com/gregario/mtg-oracle) offers `find_combos` but no bracket estimator, [nccurry/mtg-mcp](https://github.com/nccurry/mtg-mcp) exposes three Spellbook tools with no ranking, and the healthiest codebase, [nathanmartins/mtg-mcp](https://github.com/nathanmartins/mtg-mcp), has no Spellbook support. [kscius/mtg-commander-analyzer-mcp](https://github.com/kscius/mtg-commander-analyzer-mcp) hard-codes Bracket 3 rules that will go stale. None was executed during this research, and no independent security audit exists for any of them.

Because Claude Code defers MCP tool schemas, token cost is not the deciding argument ([Claude Code MCP docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/mcp)). The deciding facts are that both APIs are documented, key-less and simple; that a 100-card `estimate-bracket` response extrapolates to roughly 150 KB raw (author extrapolation from a 9.5 KB six-card sample), so a wrapper must filter to `bracketTag`, flagged cards and `combos`; and that wrappers have already failed on Scryfall rate limits and a bulk-format change. The recommendation is a small local script: download the Scryfall oracle bulk file and resolve names locally, `POST` the list to `/estimate-bracket` with a descriptive `User-Agent` at a client rate well under 80 requests per minute, back off on 429, and optionally `POST` to `/find-my-combos` for near-miss combos. Pin any community MCP to an exact version if one is used at all.

## Conclusion

The most useful finding for implementation is how little of the official system is mechanical. Of the signals the system uses (the Game Changers list plus three barometers: two-card combos, extra turns and mass land denial), only Game Changers is an enumerable list with an exact count rule; the rest are intent-based categories whose "quantities" and "early" boundaries Wizards has repeatedly declined to define, and tutors were deliberately removed from the rules to keep the system simple. A checker therefore has to be built as a floor calculator with explicit provenance per rule (official, curated list, or heuristic), and its output should carry `as_of: 2026-02-09`, `beta: true` and the Game Changer list version, not claim an exact bracket.

Spellbook is valuable precisely where Wizards is silent, since it classifies assembled combos, but its design choices (mana-value speed proxy, commander excluded from combo size, no Bracket 5, leniency toward expensive two-card combos) mean it should feed the checker as evidence rather than as the verdict. The largest open risk is time: Wizards promised a post-MagicCon revision and a possible end of the beta, the next Banned and Restricted announcement lands on 2026-10-12, and Wizards has stated that Commander unbans become Game Changers by default. Pointing the snapshot at Scryfall's `is:gamechanger` rather than a hard-coded list is the cheapest hedge against that.
